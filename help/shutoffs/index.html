Shutoffs and pins

When a pipe bursts, the one thing that matters is where the valve is. Write it down now, pin it where you stand, and it’s one tap from Home when you need it.

Shutoffs

Tap the red Shutoffs button near the top of Home. They are grouped by water, electric, gas and propane, septic and irrigation, and each card leads with where it is.

To add one: Add › A shutoff, or A shutoff here on a building’s page. Type what it shuts off — main water, gas, main breaker — or add your own kind, like a sprinkler zone. Then write:

  • Where it is, for someone who has never seen it, in the dark: “left of the front steps, under the round lid”.
  • How it turns, and whether it needs a key or a wrench.
  • What it doesn’t cover: “the shop has its own panel”.
  • Does it turn? A valve nobody has turned in years often won’t. Each shutoff gets a yearly check-up to try it. The gas shutoff’s check is that the wrench is with it, not to turn it, since shutting gas off means relighting the pilots.

A shutoff is a thing, so it has its own page: Something broke, who serviced it, and its history.

A thing that has a shutoff

Some shutoffs belong to something else: the switch in the septic pump’s control box, the breaker for the well pump. Open that thing and use Add › Its shutoff. It shows on the thing’s page and on the Shutoffs screen. The septic control box also gets a check-up to test its high-water alarm.

Pins: marking where something is

Stand at it and tap Mark where I’m standing. Shutoffs have it, and so does every thing and building — walk the property once and pin every hose bib as you go. The pin says how exact it is (“to within 6 m”).

Take me there opens the pin in Apple Maps or Google Maps for walking directions. Nothing is sent to either until you tap it.

With no signal

  • Everything you’ve written is on your phone, so the Shutoffs screen opens with no signal.
  • Marking a pin works too: the phone’s location comes from satellites, not the internet. It can take a little longer and be less exact. The pin is saved on the phone and goes up when the signal is back.
  • Take me there needs the maps app, which may not show the map without signal. That is why the written Where it is comes first.
