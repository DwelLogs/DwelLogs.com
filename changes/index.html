What has changed.

Changes go out in batches while this is being built. Newest first, with the version and build each one carries — the app shows you the same, so a report can name it.

Being worked on

A direction, not a date.

  • Ask for help: put one of your tasks out to the whole place, and anybody can take it
  • Staying a while: access that ends on its own on the day somebody leaves, and taking somebody off a place yourself
  • A room that is somebody's, for a lodger, and things that are one person's, for roommates
  • A weekend board: Today, This week, Next week
  • Renting: say you rent, and the landlord's tasks show as theirs, with Tell them or Do it myself
  • Checked, not yet: for a check-up that's a look, say it didn't need doing and when to look again
  • Seeing what the next month and quarter will cost
  • Photos and files, once there are accounts to hang them off

Known, and not fixed yet

  • There is nowhere to put a photo or a file. A paper is a link to wherever it already lives, and that is all it is for now.
  • Costs only count what's been recorded. Nothing is projected forward yet, so the number is history rather than a forecast.
  • The email digest and the calendar feed still need a Google Sheet; for accounts they are being built.
  • On a phone, everything lives in that browser. Add it to your home screen and it stays; in Safari without doing that, it clears after about a week of not opening it.
