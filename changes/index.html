What has changed.

Changes go out in batches while this is being built. Newest first, with the version and build each one carries — the app shows you the same, so a report can name it.

Being worked on

A direction, not a date.

  • Photos and files, once there are accounts to hang them off
  • Seeing what the next month and quarter will cost
  • Bringing a log back in from an export, not just sending one out
  • A weekend board — drag what you'll actually get to
  • Searching across the whole place, not one thing at a time
  • Renaming animals (people and buildings already can be)

Known, and not fixed yet

  • There is nowhere to put a photo or a file. A paper is a link to wherever it already lives, and that is all it is for now.
  • A check-up you skip on the opt-in screen is set up but kept quiet — there's no screen yet that shows you the quiet ones or turns them back on.
  • Costs only count what's been recorded. Nothing is projected forward yet, so the number is history rather than a forecast.
  • On a phone, everything lives in that browser. Add it to your home screen and it stays; in Safari without doing that, it clears after about a week of not opening it.

2026-09-30v0.3.0 · e7bb5d675b

New

  • This page: what has changed, what is known to be wrong, and what is coming next. It's in the app and on the site.
  • Something not right, or missing? There's an address in the app and on the site, and a report from the app says which build you're on.
  • The app asks your browser to hold on to your log, and says plainly what that browser will do with it.
  • A paper is a link to wherever it already lives — the maker's site, your own Drive, your email.

Fixed

  • Papers on a thing showed as "Paper" with no name or date, and tapping one opened a blank form. They show their name and when they run out, and open where they live.
  • Right after an update, the app could act up for one launch. It doesn't now.

Gone for now

  • Adding a photo. It comes back with accounts, so your pictures have somewhere proper to live.

2026-09-293f44b6d28d

New

  • Home is tiles: Buildings, Rooms and things, Animals, Vehicles and What feeds it. Open one to see only its jobs.
  • Rooms and What's in it work per building, with a switcher, so the barn's answers stay with the barn.
  • A job can say what it's about: a building, a room, a thing, an animal or a vehicle.
  • Say when something was last done, even before you had the app. The next one counts from then, not from today.
  • A catch-up list after setup, and again whenever you add a thing.
  • Marking something done takes a date — say Friday, even if you're only logging it on Sunday.
  • For past work, say who did it: not sure, you, whoever lived here before, or a company.
  • A job can list what to buy, with a part number and a link, who you call for it, and a step somewhere else — the hardware shop, the county office.
  • People and companies in one list — the septic company, the vet. Phone, what they look after, every visit, what it cost, and a note on whether you'd have them back.
  • Rename a person.

Fixed

  • Something with no due date showed as a day late. It shows as no date.
  • Answers typed for one building showed up on another.
  • What's in it opened the wrong screen and didn't save.
  • Attaching something to a job lost anything you'd typed and not saved yet.
  • On a home-screen install, the top of each screen sat under the clock.
