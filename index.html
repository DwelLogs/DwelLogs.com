Keep a pulse on your home.

Everything your home needs, before it needs it. Add things as you touch them — there's no setup evening to get through — and DwelLogs keeps the vital signs: the check-ups, the readings, the supplies, the people who help, and what it's all going to cost. The point is a home where fewer things are a surprise.

  • Regular check-upsFilters, flushes, septic pump-outs, pool care — including the ones nobody told you about.
  • Readings, not guessesSoftener salt, pool chemicals, propane and heating fuel, tracked by what you actually use, so you never run out.
  • What's wrong, and what you decidedBroken, limping, or just annoying — with the workaround, what fixing it costs, and what waiting costs. A job you put off reads as a decision, not neglect.
  • See the costs comingWhat the next month and quarter will take, worked out from what you've actually paid and how often things come round — and every figure shows where it came from.
  • Not all in one person’s headWhoever you share it with sees what needs doing, how long it takes and why it matters, and can just get on with it. The reasons are already written, so nobody has to explain them.
  • For wherever you dwellHouses, condos, rentals, ranches, ADUs, tiny houses on wheels — and the animals.
  • Yours to keepExport everything, any time. Move out, and your log moves with you. Free.
