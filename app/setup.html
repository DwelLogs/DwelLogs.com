Keeping it across devices

You do not need any of this to try DwelLogs. This is only for keeping your log on more than one device, or sharing it with somebody.

Try it first. Open the app and, when it asks for a sheet, leave both boxes blank. Everything saves on that device and nothing leaves it.

The only thing you give up is the second device. Come back here when you want that.

What this does

Your log lives in a Google Sheet you own, in your Drive. DwelLogs is a page that reads and writes it. Nobody else has a copy, there is no account, and if you ever want out you already have the whole thing in a spreadsheet.

Setting it up

  1. Make a new Google Sheet. Call it whatever you like.
  2. Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Paste in Code.gs and Schema.gs (ask Randi for these — they are two files of code).
  4. Run setUpSheets once. It builds a tab per kind of thing. Google will ask for permission the first time; it is asking on your behalf, for your own sheet.
  5. Deploy → New deployment → Web app. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone. Copy the URL it gives you.
  6. Open the app, paste that URL, and leave the token blank unless you set one.

Why “Execute as me”. The other option — “user accessing the web app” — sounds safer and does not work: the browser gets a sign-in redirect instead of an answer, and the request dies before it arrives. The script runs as you because it is your sheet.

Adding somebody else

From inside the app: Invite somebody. It gives you a link that carries the connection as well as the invite, so they tap it and they are in — no address to paste and no code to type.

That link is the key to the sheet. Send it to them the way you would send a house key: a message to them, and nowhere else. You can revoke an invite from the same screen.

Your own second device

From inside the app: My other device. Same idea, and it carries which person you are, so your phone opens as you rather than as somebody new.

When the app asks to update your sheet

DwelLogs grows, and new parts of it need columns your sheet does not have yet. When that happens a card appears on the home screen and one tap adds them.

It only ever adds. Columns you added yourself are left exactly where they are, nothing is reordered, and nothing is ever removed.

This is an early test. Things will move. Tell Randi what breaks.
